About Lunavell

Designed for the life we actually live.

Lunavell is a London-based retail brand focused on thoughtful objects for modern living — practical, considered and designed to make everyday routines feel a little easier.

01
Born in London

A new retail brand shaped by modern life.

Lunavell was created in London with a simple idea: everyday objects can be more useful, more considered and more beautiful without becoming complicated.

We are building a modern retail brand around the small things that quietly shape how a day feels — the objects we reach for, the routines we repeat and the spaces we return to.

02
Why We Exist

Better everyday objects can create better everyday moments.

Modern life is already full of noise, friction and unnecessary complexity. We do not believe the objects around us should add more of it.

Lunavell exists to identify simple opportunities to make ordinary routines feel more comfortable, more functional and more intentional.

Not by asking people to completely change the way they live, but by improving the things they already live with.

03
What We Create

Thoughtful products for everyday living.

Our collection is built around useful, design-led products that sit naturally within modern homes and modern routines.

We look for the balance between function, comfort, simplicity and visual clarity — products that solve real needs while still feeling considered.
